# Week 1 Reflection

This week, I worked on using URLs and learning how to clone a repository. The part that gave me the most trouble was cloning because I was still getting used to the steps and understanding how everything connected. I also learned what a repository is: a place where project files and their history are stored and managed.

One thing I noticed during this work is how useful AI can be. AI helped me understand unfamiliar concepts, work through problems, and figure out the steps I needed to take. This showed me that AI can be a helpful tool when I am learning something new, as long as I use it to understand the process instead of just copying the answer.
